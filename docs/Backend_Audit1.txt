2. The 6 Root Causes of Your ₹185 Call Bill
Culprit #1: SlidingWindow() Has No Token Limits (app.py lines 1072–1076)
In app.py, ContextWindowCompressionConfig is initialized with an empty SlidingWindow():

context_window_compression=(
    types.ContextWindowCompressionConfig(
        sliding_window=types.SlidingWindow(),
    )
)

When trigger_tokens and target_tokens are omitted, Vertex AI defaults trigger_tokens to 80% of the model's maximum context window (100,000+ tokens). In a 9-minute call, your session reaches ~25,000–35,000 tokens, meaning sliding window compression never triggers once. Every turn in minute 8 or 9 re-bills 100% of the audio and text accumulated since minute 1.

Culprit #2: 200ms End-of-Turn VAD Hair-Trigger (app.py line 175)Because server-side VAD is disabled (automatic_activity_detection=types.AutomaticActivityDetection(disabled=True) at line 1067), app.py manually drives Gemini turns using activity_start and activity_end based on RNNoise speech probability. Look at line 175:   PythonVAD_SPEECH_ONSET_FRAMES = 6    # ~120 ms of sustained speech to open a turn
VAD_SILENCE_OFFSET_FRAMES = 10 # ~200 ms trailing silence before end-of-turn (was 15 = 300 ms)

200 ms is shorter than a natural human pause between words. When a student or parent speaks a normal sentence in Benglish/Hinglish ("হ্যাঁ, আমার উচ্চমাধ্যমিকে... [250ms pause] ... seventy-eight percent ছিল... [250ms pause] ... Science stream-এ"), VAD_SILENCE_OFFSET_FRAMES = 10 does the following:   At the first 200 ms breath pause, it fires speech_ended = True and sends activityEnd (line 1387), forcing Gemini to bill a full context turn and start generating audio.   100 ms later, the caller speaks their next word. Because VAD_SPEECH_ONSET_FRAMES_WHILE_SPEAKING = 4 (80 ms, line 104), app.py triggers a barge-in (clearAudio), discards the audio Gemini just generated (which you already paid $12/1M output tokens for), and sends a new activityStart (line 1357).   When the caller finishes the sentence, app.py sends activityEnd again—billing the entire context window 2 to 3 times for a single user sentence. (This is also the primary reason your logs show < 350ms anomalous truncations).

Culprit #3: The find_scholarships Re-Call Loop (prompt_au_v4.txt + course_catalog.py)Look at the scholarship instructions in prompt_au_v4.txt:   First, in Step 6, Neha looks up program fees via get_program_details (which takes 2 billed model turns: one to emit the filler + tool call, and one to read the tool response and speak).   Even though Neha already collected the caller's marks in Step 4, the prompt tells her to ask permission first ("Shall I check what you could get?"), wait for the user to say yes (another turn), and then call find_scholarships (2 more billed turns).   Then, the prompt instructs: "Ask at most two [ask_about questions], one at a time, calling again with each answer... Put facts the caller says no to, or does not have, in ruled_out".   The Cost Leak: When a student says "No, I didn't give JEE Main", Gemini calls find_scholarships(program=..., qualifying_pct=..., ruled_out=["jee_main_rank"]). Calling the tool when the user says "No" cannot possibly change their best scholarship—it only burns 2 full context re-billing turns to return the exact same scholarship object!   Furthermore, in course_catalog.py (lines 442–444), every time find_scholarships matches, it appends the full static general_conditions (rules) and pitch_lines arrays into the JSON response: 

if matched:

    out["pitch_lines"] = [line["text"] for line in SCHOLARSHIPS["pitch_lines"] if line["verified"]]

Calling find_scholarships 3 times injects 3 duplicate copies of rules and pitch_lines into your session history.

As your own comment at line 68 notes, Bengali and Devanagari scripts consume 1 token per 1.8 to 2.5 characters (~2x higher token density than English).   Duplicate Post-Call STT Job: At line 1216 (finish_transcript), app.py also launches a separate Vertex batch transcription job (call_transcript.start_job) on the caller's audio turns (stt_cost_usd), meaning you are paying for caller speech transcription twice.   Uncapped Thinking Tokens: LiveConnectConfig (line 1050) does not cap thinking_config. Any internal reasoning tokens generated before tool calls or responses are billed at $4.50 / 1M tokens inside response_tokens_details (TEXT modality).
