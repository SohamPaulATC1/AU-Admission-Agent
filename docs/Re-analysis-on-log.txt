Add a Minimum RMS Energy Floor for Barge-In (app.py line 1235):
In call.log, genuine speech from your mic had inbound_rms_db between -12.3 dB and -32.6 dB, whereas many false echo/noise barge-ins fired at -41.2 dB to -64.0 dB (e.g., -56.7 dB at 12:56:12, -44.9 dB at 12:56:22, -52.1 dB at 12:57:05, -64.0 dB at 12:58:24) because RNNoise outputs high speech_prob even on very quiet residual speech.
In app.py (line 1235), require rms_db to be above -38.0 dB when assistant_speaking is True:

    active_threshold = VAD_THRESHOLD_WHILE_SPEAKING
    active_onset_frames = VAD_SPEECH_ONSET_FRAMES_WHILE_SPEAKING
    min_speech_rms_db = -38.0  # Reject faint -40dB to -64dB echo/noise while AI speaks

    active_onset_frames = VAD_SPEECH_ONSET_FRAMES

if avg_prob > active_threshold and rms_db > min_speech_rms_db:
    call_state["rnnoise_speech_count"] += 1

Widen ECHO_LAG_SEARCH_MS from 80 to 160 and Lower ECHO_CORR_THRESHOLD from 0.88 to 0.62 (app.py lines 136–138):
In call.log, every single echo leak while Neha was speaking had corr between 0.60 and 0.863 at lags pinned against the $\pm 80\text{ ms}$ boundary (lag_ms=60, 70, 80). Fix aec1.py Never Promoting (promotions=0) (aec1.py lines 53–55):

Lowering MU_MAX = 0.5 and setting PROMOTE_BELOW_INPUT = 0.85 allows aec1.py to actually promote weights and enables _suppress_residual().
